1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NextCampus AI is an institutional campus copilot engineered specifically for
Mewar University students, faculty, and administrative staff. Unlike generic,
unconstrained chatbots that hallucinate schedules and policies, NextCampus AI
is strictly grounded in verified database records (MongoDB + Motor, with resilient
in-memory fallbacks) and authoritative university ordinances.

The assistant delivers direct, truthful, and context-aware responses across 12
campus domains, resolving natural conversational follow-ups, relative dates in the
Asia/Kolkata timezone, and multilingual interactions in English, Hindi, and Hinglish.


2. CORE CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Academic Timetable Intelligence:
   - Full schedule lookup by department, course, semester, section, and weekday.
   - Next lecture determination based on current Indian Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata).
   - Classroom locations with building codes and instructor assignments.
   - Zero hallucination: states clearly when a schedule or lecture is unrecorded.

B. Hostel Dining & Mess Menu System:
   - 7-day cyclical dining roster for Central Mess and hostel dining wings.
   - Distinct meal services: Breakfast (07:30–09:15), Lunch (12:30–14:30),
     Evening Snacks (17:00–18:15), and Dinner (19:45–21:45).
   - Distinguishes broad queries ("today mess menu") from specific meals ("today's lunch").
   - Resolves relative day names ("tomorrow", "kal", "Monday", "this weekend").

C. Campus Spatial Navigation & GIS Wayfinding:
   - Step-by-step turn-by-turn routing based on authentic satellite coordinates.
   - ADA-compliant step-free routing across skybridges and ramps.
   - Covers all key facilities: Alan Turing Hall (ENG-02), Maharana Pratap Block (ADM-01),
     Central Library (LIB-03), Central Mess (CAF-05), Gate 1 (NH-48), Gate 2,
     Mewar Campus Temple & Meditation Shrine, Parking Zone, Football Ground, Hostels.

D. University Policies & Academic Ordinances:
   - Attendance Ordinance §4.1: Mandatory 75% rule, medical condonation procedures
     (65%–74.9% via Mewar University Hospital certificates), detention threshold (<65%).
   - Central Library Ordinance §2.4: 4 books (UG) / 6 books (PG), 14-day loan duration,
     ₹2/day overdue fine, 24/7 reading commons.
   - Academic Calendar & Datesheets: Mid-Term Theory (Oct 20–25), Practical Vivas (Nov 24–28),
     End-Term Examinations (Dec 1–15), Winter Break (Dec 18–Jan 4).
   - Zero-Emission Electric Campus Shuttle Transit: Route A & B stops, 10–15 min frequency.

E. Administrative Updates & Safe Publishing Workflow:
   - File ingestion supporting Excel (.xlsx via openpyxl), CSV (.csv), and PDF (.pdf).
   - Automated conflict detection: catches overlapping rooms, duplicate times, and format errors.
   - Validation preview before changes take effect.
   - Atomic publication with explicit confirmation requirement.
   - Complete version history with instant rollback capability.
   - Immutable audit logging tracking who updated what, when, and from what version.
   - Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Write operations restricted to admin role.


3. RESPONSE DESIGN & BEHAVIORAL POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tone: Professional, direct, respectful, and helpful.
- Adaptive Length: Single-paragraph concise answers for simple factual questions;
  structured markdown bulleted summaries for complex policies or timetables.
- No Artificial Fluff: Omits repetitive robotic greetings on every message turn.
- Multilingual Naturality: Speaks the user's language (English, Hindi, or Hinglish)
  without broken translation artifacts.
- Interactive Rich Cards: Returns structured JSON cards (route, timetable, mess menu,
  policy, care ticket) that render natively in the NextCampus React chat workspace.
